BishkekPM

A next-day and week-ahead smog (PM2.5) forecast for Bishkek. Student prototype, not health advice.

Forecast for the week ahead (live)

Type today's average PM2.5 for Bishkek from any air-quality app (AQICN, AQ.kg, IQAir). The page downloads the current weather forecast and runs the trained model here in your browser, so the dates move forward every day.

Loading the model from the notebook...

Why you have to type the reading: the US Embassy monitor that the model was trained on stopped reporting in 2025, so today's value cannot be downloaded automatically. With one reading, and a weather forecast in place of the real weather, expect larger errors than in the tests, especially 4 to 7 days ahead.

Replay of past days

Pick an evening between 2020 and 2024. The page shows the warning the model would have sent for the next day, and what the air turned out to be. Every forecast is for a day the model had never seen.
